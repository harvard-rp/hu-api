## Sheet modes and proposed submission fields

Players choose an inline sheet or an external Canva, Google Docs or Google Sites sheet. Both modes keep fictional identity and campus reservation data structured. The following submission matrix is proposed. Drafts may be incomplete.

Keep character ID, owner reference, type, name, applicable program and scholarship, slot occupancy and any membership or reservation references in PostgreSQL. Ownership is derived from authentication. Club reservations remain separate structured relations under their own eligibility rules. The proposal keeps narratives and face claim in the linked document for external mode and makes structured content warnings available in both modes. Reviewers check that external identity agrees with the structured fields. House, reputation, use status and HRP grants are outside owner-editable fields.

### Editorial lines

Physical description requires **at least five lines**. Personality requires **five to ten inclusive**. Backstory requires **ten to fifteen inclusive**. Propose counting explicit author line breaks. Normalize CRLF and CR to LF, ignore whitespace-only lines and count the remaining lines without counting viewport wraps. An editor can show the count and explain explicit breaks. A long wrapped paragraph counts as one line. Reviewers check that lines contain meaningful prose and check external sheets manually. Keep technical size limits separate from editorial policy. Confirm the definition for headings, decorative lines and lists. There is no conversion to words or characters.

### External content and review evidence

Propose HTTPS view links validated against an approved provider host and path list. Reject embedded credentials, deceptive hostnames and short links. Open the external document manually with clear provider labeling and no referrer. This workflow permits no provider fetch, automated download, iframe, scraping or access to users' external accounts. Inaccessible documents can be returned for correction without asking for passwords, edit permissions or forced public publication.

A view URL can point to content that is later changed or unshared. Record the submitted URL, reviewer attestation, timestamp and an optional visible provider version label. These records identify the reference used for review and cannot establish that the content remains identical. Google Docs version history can require edit permission. [Google Docs version history](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/190843?hl=en)

Propose treating a URL replacement or mode change as a sheet revision requiring resubmission under the applicable edit rules. Edits at the same URL are not automatically detectable. Propose disclosure of material changes and a staff-managed recheck when a change or loss of access is reported. Decide whether approval requires a manually verifiable immutable provider link and what to do when approved content changes. An immutable evidence workflow would need separate authorization.

## Exhaustive transition table

The review decisions are approval, correction requested and refusal. A refusal adds one to the lifetime count. Corrections never add a refusal. Approval, submission, archive and reopening do not add refusals. Preserve all decisions and player-facing comments. Archival and reopening are lifecycle events. Use status stays independent of review state. Unlisted transitions are denied. Reads leave state unchanged.

Self-review, owner reopening, and ordinary editing of submitted, approved or archived sheets are denied. Only a final student approval can make a new House assignment. Approval of other types, correction and refusal cannot make one.

### Reopening proposal

Use a single pending request per archive cycle. Allow the owner to supply a reason and explicitly authorized staff to approve or decline the request. Propose a separate characters.reopen capability with no automatic role mapping and no self-action. Keep lifetime refusals and append the reason, actor, timestamp, sheet revision and archive-cycle reference to the audit trail. The authorization allows one additional review path, including corrections. The next refusal archives immediately, and approval closes the authorization. A subsequent reopening requires a fresh staff decision and slot. Bind grants to the character and archive cycle. Replays, edits, URL replacement and mode changes never reset the count or create an authorization. Consider staff review of deliberate cloning or replacement as a separate anti-evasion policy. Shared names alone establish no evasion. Decide cooldown, repeat reopenings and whether requests may queue while full. No automatic permanent deletion.

## Capacity and transactional security

Total capacity is five plus one per active confirmed boost. Draft, submitted, correction, approved and first or second refusal occupy a slot. An archive after the refusal threshold occupies zero. A staff-reopened draft occupies one even with three or more lifetime refusals. Count by archive disposition rather than raw count. Suspension does not release a nonarchived slot. Boost loss preserves existing characters and their data and clamps availability to zero if over capacity.

Propose using the existing player capacity transaction lock for create, reopen, archive and boost confirmation. When both are needed, lock player capacity before the character row. Check session, ownership, live permissions, current revision, occupancy and archive cycle after any wait. Use fresh reads under READ COMMITTED. Preserve the existing shared House row lock and recheck that the House is active during final approval. Use identical lock ordering for operator writes. Commit a decision, review row, count, archive state, pointers and future event together. Stable operation keys scoped to caller, character and action, uniqueness constraints and revision checks should prevent double decisions, double reopening and duplicate events. Reusing a key with a different payload fails. A denied transaction leaves the count and occupancy intact.

Keep RLS, verified sessions and bounded RPCs with no client access to private schemas. Use explicit HRP capabilities from the database rather than player-controlled claims. Apply the same guards to direct RPC calls. Limit external URL and audit visibility. Escape player text and keep URLs, comments and credentials out of logs.

## Migration and API proposal

1. Inventory legacy states and derive counts from historical refusal decisions. Reconcile cases such as already-approved characters with three historical refusals and Houses assigned during correction. Preserve existing records, decisions, references and use states. Resolve grandfathering explicitly before changing any state, occupancy or House. Do not infer an archive from a historical count alone.
2. Add independent archive metadata, a compatibility default for inline mode, validated external URL evidence and append-only archive and reopening records. Derive the lifetime count from immutable reviews or maintain a transactional derived count with reconciliation. Preserve existing IDs and references. Stage a backfill and dry-run integrity and capacity checks before switching read and write paths together.
3. Propose additive list/detail responses for mode, refusal count, archive disposition and allowed actions. Return private external links only on authorized detail views. Preserve the three decision codes and separate use state. Use a typed lifecycle timeline or separate endpoint. Decide archived filtering and the GET /api/v1/me compatibility plan before rollout. Older clients lock refused sheets and will need coordinated updates.
4. Extend submit and decide contracts for mode, comment, revisions and refusal handling. Propose owner reopening-request and staff resolution endpoints. Keep the error envelope and document outcomes for invalid submission (422), stale revision, archive or capacity conflict (409), missing capability (403) and hidden foreign records (404). Match OpenAPI and direct PostgREST behavior. Version or stage incompatible changes and never rely on frontend flags to enforce security.

## Notifications and HU-010A

Propose versioned events for approval, correction requested, refusal, archive and reopening. A threshold refusal produces linked refusal and archive events in one transaction. Use a transactional outbox in a later implementation. Record event ID, character reference, type, time and revision and exclude private comments and URLs from notification payloads. Coordinate delivery ownership, recipients, permitted channels, retries, deduplication and possible coalescing of threshold notices with HU-010A. Commit state even when Discord is unavailable. No events, delivery or Discord synchronization are implemented here.

## Acceptance scenarios

- Exercise both modes and all four types. Verify structured fields, invalid URLs, absent or inapplicable fields, inaccessible external sheets and no automated provider access. Test editorial boundaries, CRLF, whitespace and changing viewport width.
- Verify that review records identify what the reviewer saw. Exercise corrections for mismatched identity and the proposed handling of an edited or inaccessible approved document.
- Exercise each transition. Deny unlisted transitions, self-review and owner reopening. Verify mandatory comments, corrections adding zero refusals, first and second refusal edits, third refusal archival and full history.
- Verify five slots plus boosts, archive releasing one, reopening acquiring one and suspension having no slot effect. Race create against reopen for the last slot, competing requests to reopen, retry of a third-refusal write and a boost reduction against a slot acquisition.
- Require an active House for student approval. Deny House writes on corrections, refusals and nonstudents. Race House deactivation with approval. Validate historical assignment references without modifying old reviews.
- Cover stale revisions, replay with a changed payload, rollback, revoked grants during lock waits, direct PostgREST and forged claims. Verify deduplicated events and committed decisions when Discord delivery fails.
- Rehearse backfill on mixed legacy data. Verify occupant counts, preserved suspension and history, and coordinated behavior across old and new APIs and UI.
